Long-Term Vision

Waxaan kula talin lahaa WG inuu noqdo:

Discord + Tournament Platform + Esports Federation Hub + Media Network

Markaas qof kasta oo Somali gamer ah markuu maqlo eFootball ama esports, meesha ugu horreysa ee uu tagayo waxay noqotaa WG.gg ama WaryaaGaming.com.

Features-ka ugu muhiimsan ee aan marka hore dhisi lahaa:

1. Discord Login
2. Tournament Registration
3. Automatic Brackets
4. Player Profiles
5. Rankings
6. Match Results
7. News Section
8. Media Hub

Kuwaas ayaa WG ka dhigi kara community-ga ugu weyn ee Somali eFootball iyo esports guud ahaan.
